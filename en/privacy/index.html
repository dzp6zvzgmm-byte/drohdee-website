2. When we are responsible and when we process for a store

DrohDee is a tool for stores. We are responsible for the data of the people who create an account and of the stores that sign up. The data each store enters about its employees and customers we process on the store’s behalf: the store decides what it enters and why, and is responsible to those people.

3. Data we collect

a) Account and store

  • Name, email address and password (the password is stored encrypted; we can’t see it).
  • When creating a store: the store’s name, a contact phone or WhatsApp number and the country.
  • Store details you add: branches and addresses, logo, receipt details and settings.
  • Devices signed in (device name and type, dates used) so you can see and sign out your sessions.
  • Your App preferences (language, light or dark mode, text size, notices).

b) Running the store (on the store’s behalf)

  • Products, prices, costs, stock, product photos, suppliers, orders and supplier bills.
  • Sales, returns, layaways, cash closes, expenses and reports.
  • Employees: name, role and permissions, invitation email, PIN (stored encrypted), schedules, attendance and, if the store uses it, pay and payments.
  • Customers: name and phone number when the store enters them to send receipts, layaways or store credit.

c) Location

When you check in or check out, the App reads the device’s location (if you allow it) and keeps the coordinates and accuracy with that record. If the store turns on location check-in, it also reads it while you work to confirm you are at the branch, and keeps each check. We don’t use location for anything else or track it outside those moments. You can deny the permission in your iPhone or iPad settings; the store may then not allow check-in.

d) Camera and photos

The App uses the camera to scan barcodes and labels and to take photos of products, the logo or the team, and can open your photo library to choose them. Code scanning happens on the device; only the photos you choose to save are uploaded. It can also save the social media images you make to your photos.

e) Website

drohdee.com uses no cookies, ads or analytics. The hosting provider may log technical data (such as the IP address) for security.

We don’t collect sensitive personal data. We don’t ask for card details: the App doesn’t process payments.

4. What we use your data for

Necessary purposes

  • Creating and managing your account and store, and letting you sign in.
  • Providing the App’s features: sales, stock, cash, team, reports and the others you use.
  • Sending service emails: account confirmation, password changes and invitations.
  • Keeping the location of check-in and check-out, and checking it while you work when the store turns it on.
  • Security, fraud prevention, backups and legal obligations.
  • Answering your support and data rights requests.

Secondary purposes

We currently don’t use your data for secondary purposes (such as advertising or marketing). If we ever do, we’ll tell you and you can refuse by writing to soporte@drohdee.com.

6. How long we keep it

While your account or the store is active. If you delete your account, your sign-in and the stores where you are the only owner are deleted, with their photos and files; your name may remain in other stores’ history (for example, sales you made as an employee). Some data may be kept as long as the law requires, or in backups for [backup period] before it is deleted.

  • Your name and your account email (or another way to reply to you).
  • A document proving your identity or, if applicable, your representation.
  • A clear description of the data and the right you want to exercise.

We’ll answer within [20 business days] and, if it applies, carry it out within the following [15 business days] [lawyer to confirm the periods]. 8. Security

We use encrypted connections, passwords and PINs stored encrypted, and database rules so each person sees only what their store and role allow. No system is perfect; if a breach affects your rights, we’ll tell you as the law requires.

9. Minors

The App is meant for businesses and people over 18. We don’t knowingly collect data from minors.
